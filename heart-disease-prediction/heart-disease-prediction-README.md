# Heart Disease Prediction

Predicts the likelihood of heart disease from patient clinical data.

## Dataset
Heart disease dataset sourced from Kaggle (`heart_disease_data.csv`).

## Approach
- Data cleaning and feature preparation from patient health indicators
- Trained a **Logistic Regression** classifier

## Results
| Metric | Score |
|---|---|
| Testing Accuracy | 88.52% |

## Tech Stack
Python, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib, seaborn
